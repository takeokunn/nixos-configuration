Drafting patterns for technical prose. Read the reference that matches the work:

- `references/article-types.md`: choosing an article type (tutorial, concept explanation, comparison, case
  study, opinion piece), writing principles, title patterns, and English and bilingual tone.
- `references/prose-and-argument.md`: any human-facing prose, including a report, a PR/issue body, or
  documentation; procedural versus descriptive passages, paragraphs, argument, reader load, redundancy,
  headings, and honesty to the reader.
- `references/long-form.md`: book chapters and multi-part series; read it together with the other two.

Japanese prose rules (notation, punctuation, sentence length and endings, wording to avoid): see the yomiyasu
skill. When a yomiyasu revision pass and these references disagree, yomiyasu decides.

Wording to cut in any prose (announcements, restatements, intensifiers, formulaic contrast, em dash, emoji,
praise): see the prose rules of the explanatory-strict output style. When to load this skill, yomiyasu, and
cold-read: see CLAUDE.md output_discipline.
